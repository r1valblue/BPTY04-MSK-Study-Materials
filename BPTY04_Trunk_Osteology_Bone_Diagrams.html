Regional Vertebral Characteristics

Cervical (C3–C7) — Superior View

1Vertebral body — small
2Vertebral foramen — large, triangular
3Spinous process — short & bifid (C3–C6); long (C7)
4Transverse Foramen (key: for vertebral artery)
5Transverse Process (ant. & post. tubercles)
6Articular facets — flat, ~45° from horizontal

Thoracic — Lateral View

1Vertebral body — medium
2Superior costal facet (for rib head)
3Inferior costal facet (for rib head below)
4Transverse process costal facet (for rib tubercle)
5Spinous process — long, angled inferiorly
6Superior articular process (60–80° from horizontal)
7Inferior articular process
