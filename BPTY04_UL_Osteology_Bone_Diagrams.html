Clavicle Scapula Humerus Radius & Ulna Carpus & Hand

1. Clavicle (Clavicula)

Superior View

1Sternal (medial) end — cylindrical; articulates with manubrium via disc
2Body / shaft — elongated, S-curved; subcutaneous throughout
3Acromial (lateral) end — flat; articulates with acromion (AC joint)
4Anterior margin — deltoid (lateral) & SCM/trapezius (medial) attachments
5Posterior margin — trapezius attachment laterally

Inferior View (Attachment Sites)

1Sternal articular surface — smaller circular facet; disc interposed
2Costal tuberosity — rough medial area; costoclavicular ligament
3Subclavian groove — depression along shaft; subclavius muscle
4Conoid tubercle — junction mid-/lateral ¹⁄₃; conoid ligament
5Trapezoid line — ridge on lateral ¹⁄₃; trapezoid ligament
6Acromial articular surface — smaller flat facet; AC joint

2. Scapula

Posterior View

1Superior angle — levator scapulae attachment
2Inferior angle — latissimus dorsi attachment; level T7
3Lateral angle — glenoid fossa (humeral head)
4Superior border — shortest border
5Suprascapular notch — suprascapular nerve passes below lig.
6Medial (vertebral) border — longest border; rhomboids, serratus ant.
7Lateral (axillary) border — thickest; teres major & minor
8Spine of scapula — level T3; flat triangular → acromion; deltoid & trapezius
9Acromion — deltoid & trapezius; articulates with clavicle (AC jt)
10Supraspinous fossa — supraspinatus origin
11Infraspinous fossa — infraspinatus origin
12Neck of scapula — narrowing between body and glenoid
13Infraglenoid tubercle — long head triceps brachii origin

Anterior (Costal) View

1Subscapular fossa — large anterior concavity; subscapularis origin
2Coracoid process — pec. minor, LH biceps brachii, coracobrachialis
3Glenoid fossa — pear-shaped; articulates with humeral head
4Supraglenoid tubercle — long head biceps brachii origin
5Superior angle — levator scapulae attachment
6Inferior angle — latissimus dorsi; most inferior palpable point
7Medial (vertebral) border — rhomboids, serratus anterior
8Lateral (axillary) border — teres major & minor; LH triceps origin below
9Superior border — suprascapular notch; shortest border

3. Humerus

Anterior View

1Head — dome-shaped; faces superomedially and posteriorly
2Anatomical neck — groove around articular margin; capsule attachment
3Surgical neck — narrowing below tubercles; common fracture site
4Greater tubercle — highest point; supra/infraspinatus & teres minor
5Lesser tubercle — anterior prominence; subscapularis tendon
6Bicipital groove — sulcus intertubercularis; LH biceps brachii
7Medial crest — of bicipital groove; teres major + latissimus dorsi
8Lateral crest — of bicipital groove; pectoralis major
9Deltoid tuberosity — roughened lateral shaft; deltoid insertion
10Body / shaft — 3 borders (ant., med., lat.), 3 surfaces
11Medial epicondyle — larger; common flexor origin; ulnar nerve posterior
12Lateral epicondyle — common extensor origin; radial collateral lig.
13Trochlea — spool-shaped; articulates with trochlear notch of ulna
14Capitulum — rounded; articulates with head of radius
15Coronoid fossa — anterior; receives coronoid process in flexion
16Radial fossa — anterior; receives radial head in flexion

Posterior View

1Head — faces posteromedially; retroversion ~30°
2Greater tubercle — prominently visible from posterior
3Anatomical neck — articular margin groove
4Surgical neck — common fracture site; axillary nerve nearby
5Radial (spiral) groove — posterior shaft; radial nerve + profunda brachii a.; delimits triceps origins
6Body / shaft — 3 borders, 3 surfaces
7Olecranon fossa — large posterior pit; receives olecranon in full extension
8Medial epicondyle — larger medial bony prominence
9Ulnar nerve sulcus — posterior medial epicondyle; "funny bone"
10Lateral epicondyle — common extensor tendon origin
11Trochlea — wraps from anterior to posterior

4. Radius & Ulna — Anterior View (Anatomical / Supinated)

Anterior View — Supinated (Anatomical Position)  ·  Ulna = Medial  ·  Radius = Lateral

Blue = Ulna landmarks  ·  Red = Radius landmarks

Ulna

U1Olecranon — triceps brachii insertion; enters olecranon fossa in extension
U2Trochlear notch — C-shaped articular surface; articulates with trochlea
U3Coronoid process — triangular; brachialis insertion; resists posterior dislocation
U4Radial notch — lateral proximal ulna; articulates with radial head (PRUJ)
U5Ulnar tuberosity — rough area below coronoid; brachialis attachment
U6Crest of supinator — proximal interosseous border; supinator origin
U7Interosseous margin — sharp lateral ridge; interosseous membrane attachment
U8Head of ulna — distal, small; articulates with TFCC disc + ulnar notch of radius (DRUJ)
U9Styloid process — posterior medial; ulnar collateral ligament

Radius

R1Head of radius — disc-shaped; articulates with capitulum (humeroradial jt) + radial notch of ulna (PRUJ)
R2Neck of radius — narrow region between head and shaft
R3Radial tuberosity — medial, below neck; biceps brachii tendon insertion
R4Interosseous margin — sharp medial ridge; interosseous membrane attachment
R5Lister's tubercle (dorsal tubercle) — posterior surface; EPL tendon pulley; landmark for scaphoid/lunate
R6Ulnar notch (sigmoid notch) — medial distal radius; DRUJ; articulates with ulnar head
R7Carpal articular surface — lateral facet → scaphoid; medial facet → lunate
R8Radial styloid process — lateral distal; longer than ulnar styloid; radial collateral lig.

5. Carpus & Hand — Palmar View (Right Hand)

Palmar (Anterior) View — Right Hand  ·  Radial (thumb) side = LEFT  ·  Ulnar side = RIGHT

1Scaphoid (S) — most fractured carpal; retrograde blood supply → AVN risk
2Tubercle of scaphoid — palpable palmar; lateral wall carpal tunnel; flexor retinaculum attachment
3Lunate (L) — crescent-shaped; prone to dislocation ("spilled teacup" sign on X-ray)
4Triquetrum (Tr) — pisiform on its palmar surface; lunotriquetral lig.
5Pisiform (P) — sesamoid in FCU tendon; Guyon's canal medial wall
6Trapezium (Tm) — CMC joint of thumb; APB, FPB, opponens pollicis
7Tubercle of trapezium — palpable palmar; lateral wall carpal tunnel; flexor retinaculum
8Trapezoid (Td) — smallest distal carpal; MC2 base
9Capitate (C) — largest carpal; central keystone; articulates with 7 bones
10Hamate (H) — MC4 & MC5 bases; hypothenar muscles
11Hook of hamate — palpable palmar; medial wall carpal tunnel + Guyon's canal
Carpal Bone Mnemonics
Proximal row (lateral → medial): She Likes To Play — Scaphoid · Lunate · Triquetrum · Pisiform
Distal row (lateral → medial): Try To Catch Her — Trapezium · Trapezoid · Capitate · Hamate

Carpal tunnel walls: Lateral — tubercle of scaphoid + trapezium  ·  Medial — pisiform + hook of hamate  ·  Roof = flexor retinaculum  ·  Contains: median nerve + 9 flexor tendons
Guyon's canal: pisiform (medial) ↔ hook of hamate (lateral) — ulnar nerve and artery
